Getting started

  1. 1

    Select calculation rules

    On the welcome page or at the top of the chat, choose a mold type rule (e.g. injection / stamping) so Q&A and estimation use the correct rules.

  2. 2

    Switch Q&A mode

    Below the input box, switch between Knowledge Q&A and Cost Estimation: the former searches the knowledge base; the latter is for part parameters and cost estimation.

  3. 3

    Manage the KB Knowledge Base

    Click KB Knowledge Base on the left to upload PDFs, view documents, and maintain knowledge sources.

  4. 4

    New and past conversations

    Use New conversation to start a session; browse and switch past records in Conversation history.
